%------------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}
  SA \cite{Bertsimas1993Simulated,Henderson2003Simulated} is an optimization techniwue for finding an optimal solution to an optimization problem.
  The idea and name are coined by \emph{annealing the metallurgy}: you heat metal ans then cool it solwly.
  While it's hot, the atoms move around freely.
  As it cools solwly. they settle into a low-energy, well-ordered crystal structure.
  If you cool it too fast, the atoms get stuck in a messy, higher-enrgy arrangement.
